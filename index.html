Do guests need to download an app?+

No. Guests scan the QR with their phone camera and the menu opens in the browser, on Android and iPhone.

What if we don’t have videos of our dishes?+

Start with photos. Short clips shot on a phone work well, and NomNom resizes them so they load quickly. Begin with your 10–20 hero dishes and add more over time.

Can we change prices and mark dishes sold out ourselves?+

Yes. The dashboard updates the live menu immediately, with no reprinting.

Does it work with our POS?+

Table orders arrive in the NomNom dashboard with the table number, and can be printed as a kitchen ticket or exported. Direct POS sync is on our roadmap.

Is table ordering required?+

No. Switch ordering off and NomNom works as a view-only video menu.
